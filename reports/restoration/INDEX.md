The held-out studies retain their exact original binary/model/evaluator identities. Later correctness or packaging checks do not silently replace their quality/performance figures. Repeated calls and authored diagnostic fixtures are not independent labeled tasks. JIT/native/resident timing, stdout/diagnostic context cost and source-preservation evidence are distinct.

Early loose captures were moved without changing bytes. [PATH_MAP.json](PATH_MAP.json) records original/current paths and SHA256; [the original six-gate capture index](gate6/CAPTURE_INDEX.md) is preserved byte-for-byte. Its historical relative links assumed this directory. Newer gates keep exact local recipes/paths in their receipts. Commands requiring ignored model/corpus snapshots are research reproductions, separate from ordinary source installation.

Current Windows/Linux execution has local evidence. Native Mac remains deferred. Provider credential revocation and reviewed history remediation precede public release; no public push/history rewrite/release has been performed by these gates. Private contribution/application/chat notes and downloaded model/corpus artifacts remain excluded from Git.
